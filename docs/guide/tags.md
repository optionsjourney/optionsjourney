# Tags

*Labels that group your trades, so you can filter the Journal and save dashboards by them.*

A trade can have as many tags as you like, such as "MEIC", "Wheel" or "earnings". You choose which ones matter by filtering on them (see [Filters and dashboards](journal.md#filters-and-dashboards)). This page is about your trades' tags. Bots have labels of their own, for finding them on the Automation page (see [Tags on bots](automation.md#tags)).

## The Tags page
Click **Tags** above the trade list in the Journal (it's there on P/L and Trading metrics too). It lists every tag, A to Z, with how many trades you've given it. **← Back to the journal** takes you back.

- **Add a tag:** type its name in the box at the bottom and click **Add tag**. A name can be up to 60 characters and can't have a comma in it. A name that's already taken, in any capitals, is refused.
- **Rename** one: change the name in its box and click **Rename**. Every trade with it shows the new name.
- **Delete** one: it asks first, then the tag comes off every trade. Your trades themselves are never touched.

The tags you ticked under **How you trade** when you set up the app are there to start with (see [Setting up](setup.md)).

## Tags that come by themselves
Some tags are put on trades for you. They aren't in the page's counts, which are only the trades you've tagged yourself (or matched to a bot):
- **A bot's trades** carry the tag the bot files them under, its **Journal tag** (you pick it in the bot's Basics).
- **Stock trades** always carry **Stocks**.

**A bot's tag can't be deleted.** The page names the bot beside the count ("bot: IC Mornings"); click the name to open the bot and pick another Journal tag first. A bot you've deleted still holds its tag, shown as "(deleted)", because its past trades keep their link to it. You can still rename a bot's tag.

## Tagging trades
- **One trade:** click the gear (⚙) on it, then **Edit name, tags and notes**, and change its tags on the trade's own page (see [The trade's own page](journal.md#the-trades-own-page)). A tag you type that doesn't exist yet is made for you.
- **Several at once:** tick them in the Journal and use the bar at the bottom (see [Risk graphs](journal.md#risk-graphs), "Tags for several trades").
- **A bot's past trades:** **Match trades** on the bot's page gives them the bot's tag and links them to it (see [Finding a bot's past trades](automation.md#finding-a-bots-past-trades)).

A tag also decides which trades count in a bot's record: besides the trades it placed or that you matched to it, every trade with its tag in its account that no bot is linked to, including ones you opened yourself (see [A bot's record](automation.md#a-bots-record)).
