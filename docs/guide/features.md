# Features

Everything OptionsJourney does, in one list. Each part links to its page in the guide for the details. It works with **TastyTrade** and **Charles Schwab**, and runs on your own Mac or Windows computer.

## Trade
- **Option chain:** pick a symbol and an expiration, then click a bid to sell or an ask to buy. The legs go straight onto the ticket. The chain marks the strikes you already hold. See [Trade](trade.md).
- **Order ticket:** the price, credit or debit, and the account, with delta, chance of profit, max profit and max loss as you build.
- **Review order:** your broker checks the order and says what it would cost, including fees and buying power, before anything is sent.
- **Risk graph:** what the trade makes or loses at every price, at expiry and today. Slide time forward or try a different volatility. It also shows the expected move, breakevens and the Greeks.
- **Working orders** from all your accounts, in one list. You can change the price of, or cancel, the orders you sent from the app.
- **Ideas:** save a trade you built but didn't send, and follow its P/L. See [Ideas](ideas.md).
- **Pending orders:** every order still waiting at your broker, in every account, in one drawer on the Journal. See [Pending orders](journal.md#pending-orders).
- **Futures options** like /ES, on TastyTrade.

## Look after your trades
- **A profit target and a stop on every trade:** set them when you open it, or add them to a trade you already have. See [Manage this trade](journal.md#manage-this-trade).
- **Stops held at your broker** keep working even when your computer is off.
- **Timed closes:** close a trade at a set time, for example before the close.
- **Adjust as you go:** close part of a trade, move its stop or target, or roll a leg. See [Adjust a bot's trade before the close](how-to-adjust-before-the-close.md).

## Bots
- **Bots open trades for you** at set times on the days you pick, with their stops and targets already on. See [Automation](automation.md) and [Set up your first bot](how-to-first-bot.md).
- **Paper or Live:** a Paper bot does everything except send the order, so you can watch it for a while before trusting it. See [Go from Paper to Live](how-to-paper-to-live.md).
- **Many shapes:** iron condors, credit and debit spreads, butterflies, straddles, strangles, or your own with up to 4 legs, on SPX, XSP, stocks and ETFs.
- **Hand a trade to a bot:** a trade you opened yourself can be looked after by a bot's rules. See [Hand a trade to a bot](how-to-hand-a-trade-to-a-bot.md).
- **Share a bot** with a friend as a small file. It never includes your account, login or sizes.
- **Market calendar:** bots skip market holidays, and any blackout days you pick.

## Safety
- **Stop all bots**, always one click away.
- **No new trades** and **Freeze all orders:** two switches that pause trading, with a banner on every page while one is on. See [Pausing trading](pausing-trading.md).
- **Limits:** the most a bot can send per trade and per day, checked before every order.
- **The order log:** every order the app sends or checks, and what the broker said.

## Charts
- **Up to 10 live charts** on one screen, for any stock, ETF, index or futures. See [Charts](charts.md).
- **Your own layouts** in tabs, and pop-out windows for a second monitor.
- **Indicators:** moving averages, VWAP, Bollinger and Keltner bands, the expected move, the opening range, volume profiles, RSI and MACD.
- **Drawing tools:** lines, trend lines, boxes and Fibonacci levels, saved for each symbol.
- **Watchlist** of your own symbols, and of the ones you're trading.
- **News:** the latest headlines for the symbol on the chart, beside it. Hover over one for a short summary.

## Journal
- **Every trade, by itself:** fills from your brokers are grouped into trades, open and closed, with live P/L. See [Your trade journal](journal.md).
- **Tags and notes:** group trades your way, like "earnings" or "wheel", and filter by them. See [Tags](tags.md).
- **Dashboards:** save a set of filters and come back to it.
- **Trades at other brokers** can be added by hand.
- **Paper trades** have a view of their own and never mix with your real numbers.

## Metrics
See what's working and what isn't, on the **Metrics** tab. Every number follows the Journal's filters, so you can look at everything or at one tag, account, bot or saved dashboard. See [Metrics](metrics.md).
- **The headline numbers:** realized P/L, win rate, expectancy (what a trade makes on average), profit factor (money won for each dollar lost) and max drawdown.
- **Wins next to losses:** the average win and loss, how big wins are next to losses, the largest of each, and what you paid in fees.
- **P/L calendar:** each day in green or red, with how many trades closed, the day's big economic events, a total for each week, and the month's best and worst day.
- **A day, week or month's trades:** click one for its trades, grouped by your dashboards, with a column for each account that traded.
- **Cumulative P/L and drawdown:** two charts of your running P/L and how far below your best point you were, day by day.
- **By day, week or month:** a bar for each with a cumulative P/L line over them, plus the winners, the average, and the best and worst.
- **Day of the week:** how each weekday has gone.
- **Each bot's record:** its P/L and win rate, per lot the average trade, winner and loser, and every trade it made, with how many days it was held and its P/L as a % of the premium.

## Accounts
- **All your accounts** in one place: value, cash and buying power, and a total. See [Your accounts](accounts.md).
- **Account values over time**, on a chart.
- **Transactions:** everything the app has read from your brokers. See [Transactions](transactions.md).

## Alerts
- **The bell** shows what needs your attention, with a button to go and fix it. See [Alerts](alerts.md).
- **Your own alerts** on a price, a spread, a trade's P/L, or several trades' P/L together (the bell on a journal trade).
- **On your phone** through Discord or Telegram. See [Get alerts on your phone](how-to-phone-alerts.md).

## Yours
- **On your computer:** no subscription, no account with us, no server in between. Your broker logins stay in your computer's own password store.
- **Runs in the background** with an icon in the menu bar or system tray. See [The menu bar icon](menubar.md).
- **Use it from your phone or other computers**, at home or away. See [FAQ](faq.md).
- **Backups** every day or week, kept on your computer. See [Settings](settings.md).
- **Help** built in, with search as you type.
- **Updates** from inside the app. See [Update the app](updating.md).
