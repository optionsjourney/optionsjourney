# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help. OptionsJourney is a small project run by one person, so here's how it works.

## Ideas, questions and bug reports
Use [Discussions](https://github.com/optionsjourney/optionsjourney/discussions). For a bug, say what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Leave out account numbers, balances and anything else private.

## Security problems and private matters
Email [hello@optionsjourney.com](mailto:hello@optionsjourney.com) instead of posting in public, especially for anything that could affect someone's orders or broker logins.

## Code
- **Small fixes are welcome** (typos, clear bugs, wording in the guide).
- **For anything bigger, start a Discussion first**, so you don't spend time on something that won't fit.
- **Pull requests are applied by hand, not merged.** The work happens in a private repository, and this one gets a copy at each release. If your change is accepted, it's copied over with credit to you (a `Co-authored-by` line), your pull request is closed with a note, and the change appears in the next release.

## Changes that touch money
Anything that affects how orders are sent, the safety checks (size limits, No new trades, Freeze all orders, Paper vs Live), or broker logins is reviewed line by line and may take a while, or be declined. That's not personal; it's other people's money.
